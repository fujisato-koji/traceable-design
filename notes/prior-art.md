# Prior art

This note lists what other systems teach about sharing engineering records, verified knowledge, and operating experience.
It is the evidence behind S-20 to S-23 in [spec/01-requirements.md](../spec/01-requirements.md) and behind how the standard spreads.
The lessons (L-nn) come from a survey of 48 examples made on 2026-10-25.
Figures marked "secondhand" were taken from search summaries, not from the source page itself.

## Lessons

| ID | Lesson | Examples | Serves |
|----|--------|----------|--------|
| L-01 | The value of a shared layer comes from telling everyone who depends on an item that it changed or was wrong, with who verified it, not from storing items. | Dependabot matches a repository's dependencies against advisories ([GitHub Docs](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/dependabot/dependabot-alerts/about-dependabot-alerts)); OSV gives advisories one shape across ecosystems ([OSV](https://ossf.github.io/osv-schema/)); Overture's GERS ids link old and new ids when a road splits ([Overture](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/gers/)). | S-20, S-22 |
| L-02 | Standards that lasted were narrow, testable, and free of fees. One that tried to model a whole product life stalled. | FMI exchanges simulation models only, had checkers from the start, and was supported by over 170 tools in 2022 ([CIMdata](https://www.cimdata.com/en/industry-summary-articles/item/18637-the-modelica-association-and-fmi-project-announce-the-release-of-function-mockup-interface-fmi-v3-0)). PLCS (ISO 10303 AP239) names its own complexity and file size as obstacles ([AP239 white paper](https://www.ap239.org/c/document_library/White_Paper_AP239_ed3.pdf)). | S-13 |
| L-03 | A receiver that asks for the format drives the first use. | Carmakers asked suppliers for ReqIF ([ProSTEP](https://www.prostep.org/en/projects/reqif-project-groups)); the EU product passport drives the Asset Administration Shell ([IDTA](https://industrialdigitaltwin.org/en/dpp4-0)); Catena-X grew from 182 companies in 2025-12 to 1,069 in 2026-09 under carmaker and battery rules ([Catena-X](https://catena-x.net/news/catena-x-network-grows-by-more-than-a-factor-of-five-to-over-1000-companies/)). | spreading |
| L-04 | Feedback across organizations closed only where a lesson became a required action and the action was checked. A store alone was not used. | WANO asks every member to act on significant recommendations and checks it in peer reviews ([World Nuclear Association](https://world-nuclear.org/information-library/current-and-future-generation/cooperation-in-nuclear-power)); FAA service difficulty reports feed airworthiness directives ([GAO](https://www.gao.gov/products/RCED-91-24)); in NASA's lessons learned system, 57 % of program managers used it and 43 % contributed ([NASA OIG IG-12-012](https://www.oversight.gov/sites/default/files/oig-reports/IG-12-012.pdf)). | S-19, S-22 |
| L-05 | Competitors shared for decades what does not set them apart, through a neutral keeper and a common taxonomy. | OREDA pools equipment failure records among oil companies since 1981, and its taxonomy became ISO 14224 ([oreda.com](https://oreda.com)). | S-20 |
| L-06 | Anonymity alone did not bring reports; protection and a keeper that does not enforce did. | The chemical process incident database holds about 710 incidents ([AIChE](https://www.aiche.org/ccps/resources/psid/psid-confidentiality-liability)); NASA's aviation safety reporting system received 106,087 reports in 2023 under limited immunity ([ASRS](https://asrs.arc.nasa.gov/docs/ASRS_ProgramBriefing.pdf)). | S-21 |
| L-07 | People paid for keeping their own record, not for sharing. | CADDi ended its order marketplace in 2024-07 and grew on a drawing record service ([Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.jp/article/2503-caddi-pivot-new-investment-ceo-interview/)); Wikifactory earns from a paid tool for change orders and audit logs ([Digital Engineering](https://www.digitalengineering247.com/article/cad-rooms-engineering-platform-launches)). | S-12 |
| L-08 | Open peer-to-peer maker markets did not last; markets that choose makers by quality did. | 3D Hubs dropped individual hubs in 2018 and was bought in 2021 ([All3DP](https://all3dp.com/4/3d-hubs-3d-printing-going-fully-b2b-leave-low-activity-makers-behind/)); Xometry sets prices and reassigns work to chosen suppliers, still at a net loss in 2025 (secondhand, [GlobeNewswire](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/02/24/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/xometry-reports-record-fourth-quarter-and-strong-full-year-2025-results/2283753)). | spreading |
| L-09 | A format is necessary but not enough. | Gerber has been free since 1980; price and scale of fabs such as JLCPCB moved the network ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerber_format), [JLCPCB](https://jlcpcb.com/about-us)). | spreading |
| L-10 | Prices and part numbers kept by hand go stale first. | Kitspace aimed at one-click ordering from Gerber files and part numbers; by 2018 almost no project could really be bought in one go ([GOSH forum](https://forum.openhardware.science/t/made-in-shenzhen-documentation-of-open-science-hardware-created-during-gosh-2018/1520)). | S-23 |
| L-11 | A commons that AI reads but does not write back to shrinks. One that its users fix for their own needs stays fresh. | Stack Overflow activity fell about 25 % within six months of ChatGPT ([PNAS Nexus](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae400)); Wikipedia human page views fell about 8 % in 2025-03 to 2025-08 ([Wikimedia](https://diff.wikimedia.org/2025/10/17/new-user-trends-on-wikipedia/)); companies that depend on OpenStreetMap became its main editors ([Anderson et al.](https://www.mdpi.com/2220-9964/8/5/232)). | S-23 |
| L-12 | Rewarding volume lowered quality. | Thingiverse and Printables reward downloads and likes; the buyer of Thingiverse in 2026-02 said it would exclude AI-generated designs ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/3d-printing-saved-from-ai-as-myminifactory-acquires-thingiverse-over-8-million-users-and-an-archive-of-over-2-5-million-things-set-to-be-protected)). | S-21 |
| L-13 | No open hardware platform returns field feedback to the design as a rule. Metadata standards with no reader stalled. | Feedback came through forums and issues (OpenFlexure, [openflexure.org](https://openflexure.org/)), trials inside one organization ([Field Ready](https://fieldready.org/post/2017/07/18/scaling-up-local-production-introducing-our-new-kits-program)), or releases by a central keeper (WikiHouse, [week notes](https://wikihouse.substack.com/p/week-notes-88)); Open Know-How has thin adoption ([GitHub](https://github.com/iop-alliance/OpenKnowHow)). | S-19 |
| L-14 | AI tools for hardware design and systems engineering are closed per customer, and none keeps sources and strength per value, owner decisions, operation feedback, and reuse across projects together. | Flow Engineering raised USD 50 million at a USD 750 million valuation in 2026-10 ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/01/flow-engineering-raises-50m-to-bring-the-power-of-ai-to-hardware-engineering/)); Valispace was sold to Altium in 2024 ([SE Trends](https://www.se-trends.de/en/valispace-altium-requirements-portal/)); Onshape opened an MCP server for any assistant in 2026-08 ([PTC](https://www.placera.se/pressmeddelanden/ptc-ptc-advances-leadership-in-ai-driven-design-with-launch-of-onshape-featurescript-mcp-server-20260813)). | S-13 |
| L-15 | Most platforms fail within five years; networks split into local clusters are weaker; an open format makes moving between hosts free, so a host must earn its keep by service. | [MIT Sloan](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/digital-platforms-high-valuations-high-risk-failure); Zhu and Iansiti 2019 ([summary](https://maaw.info/ArticleSummaries/ArtSumZhuandIansiti2019.htm)). | spreading |
